Nowt

Encrypted Notes & Tasks

Nowt is a notes and tasks app for Android and the web. You write in blocks rather than one long box of text, keep your tasks and reminders next to the notes they belong to, and sync everything between your phone and your browser. Encryption is optional, and when you switch it on your content is encrypted on your own device before it leaves it.

Open the web app
app.voynich.systems. Runs in any modern browser, nothing to install.

Writing in blocks

A note is a stack of blocks you add, reorder and mix as you go, so one note can hold a heading, a checklist, a photo and a map pin without fighting the editor.

  • Paragraphs with bold, italic and highlight, plus headings, quotes, callouts, dividers, code and tables.
  • Checklists, bulleted lists and numbered lists, tickable and reorderable in place.
  • Images, audio recordings, documents, moodboards and link previews.
  • Map locations, books, films and TV, countdowns and timestamps, each as its own block.

Sync and encryption

Nowt works offline on your device. Sign in and your notes sync across the devices you have linked, so something written on your phone is waiting in the browser.

End-to-end encryption is optional and the choice is yours. With it switched on, your notes, attachments and tasks are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, and the keys stay on your devices. We cannot decrypt your content with the keys we hold.

That cuts both ways, and we would rather say so here than in the small print: recovery is on you. If you lose your password and your recovery key, the content cannot be restored by anyone, us included. With encryption switched off, your content is still encrypted at rest on our servers, but it is decryptable by the service. Either way we hold your account email, device information and subscription status, which is set out in full in the privacy policy.

Free and Pro

Nowt is free to use. A paid Pro subscription adds full access to the web app, along with reminders and repeating tasks. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, and subscriptions are billed through Google Play.
